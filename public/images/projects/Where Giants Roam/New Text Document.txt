Where Giants Roam – Creative Digital Studio Website | Immersive Web Experience for Visual Storytelling

Where Giants Roam is a Scotland-based creative studio specializing in CGI, motion design, live action, and VFX for global brands. As a Web Developer, I built a highly interactive and visually striking website that communicates the studio’s artistic vision while showcasing their award-winning projects. The site emphasizes immersive storytelling through full-screen video, smooth scrolling animations, and responsive layouts that perform seamlessly across devices. To achieve this, I implemented cutting-edge web technologies with GSAP-powered animations, custom scroll behavior, and an optimized asset pipeline for fast loading times. The result is a digital platform that balances cinematic presentation with usability, ensuring visitors engage deeply with the studio’s creative work. This project strengthened the studio’s digital identity, supported their collaborations with brands like Balvenie, Jo Malone, Brewdog, and Oculus, and contributed to industry recognition with awards from Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, FWA, and Scottish Design Awards. The website demonstrates my ability to deliver premium, interactive, and design-focused experiences that elevate a brand’s global presence.
