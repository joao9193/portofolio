We Are Found – 360 Lifestyle & Property Experience | Immersive Website for Neighbourhood Living

We Are Found is a visionary property and lifestyle operator redefining residential and commercial spaces through curated experiences. As the Web Developer, I crafted a responsive and engaging website that brings the brand’s immersive, community-first philosophy to life across devices. From the first scroll, visitors are invited into a world where "Live • Work • Experience" isn't just a tagline but a promise. I designed fluid page transitions, atmospheric imagery, and strategic information layers that reinforce the brand's message: Found doesn't just build spaces—they curate experiences. Intuitive calls to action—“Find your space,” “Find your home,” “Explore”—encourage seamless exploration of neighborhoods, residential offerings, and community events. I integrated interactive animations and responsive layouts to highlight features like on-site teams, bespoke property services, public activations, and soon-to-be-launched spaces. This structure balances immersive storytelling with clear usability—allowing both residents and commercial clients to quickly understand and connect with Found’s lifestyle-driven vision. To ensure long-term performance and flexibility, I implemented a modern frontend stack with mobile-first responsiveness and scalable design patterns. The setup ensures smooth animations, fast loading, and effortless future content updates—ideal for evolving properties and dynamic community programs. The site eloquently conveys Found's identity as a 360° lifestyle operator—communities that are more than real estate, woven with green spaces, events, and branded activations. This project showcases my ability to merge storytelling, interactive design, and technical refinement to elevate brand positioning in sectors where experience is just as essential as architecture.
