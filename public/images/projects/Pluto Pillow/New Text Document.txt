Pluto Pillow – Custom Sleep Experience Platform | Personalized Comfort for Better Sleep

Pluto Pillow is a hybrid design pillow brand that offers fully customized pillows based on each user's individual sleep style and body type. As a web developer, I built a web platform that allows users to easily purchase custom pillows tailored to their sleep profiles. The website is designed around a questionnaire-based, personalized experience. Short, intuitive questions guide users through collecting their physical data, sleeping position, and preferences. A proprietary algorithm then recommends the optimal pillow specifications—height, firmness, and material combination—and then provides the user with a personalized product based on those results. The user interface, which incorporates the customization process, is designed to foster emotional trust and immersion. Furthermore, Pluto Pillow offers a 125-day free trial, a one-year warranty, and free modifications or returns to enhance customer satisfaction. Furthermore, the overall user flow is smooth and responsive, maintaining consistent high performance across mobile and desktop platforms. The project has fueled the brand's growth since its appearance on "Shark Tank," and has even garnered recognition as "Best Customizable Pillow" from major media outlets and awards, including CNN Underscored, CNET, the Oprah Sleep Awards, and The Strategist.
